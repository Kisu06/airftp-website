Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

Introduction

Air FTP Server ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

No Personal Data Collection

Air FTP Server does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information, user data, or analytics data. The app operates entirely on your device.

Local Data Storage

The app only stores the following information locally on your device:

  • FTP server configuration settings (port, username, password, etc.)
  • Selected home folder path
  • App preferences (theme, SSL settings, etc.)

This data is stored locally in your device's internal storage and is never transmitted to any external servers.

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to use or share. All functionality of the app operates locally on your device.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions for core functionality:

  1. Storage Permissions — Required to access files from user-selected folders to serve via FTP/FTPS protocol.
  2. Network Permissions — Required for FTP server operation over WiFi.
  3. WiFi Permissions — Required to check WiFi status and enable WiFi if needed.
  4. Notification Permission — Required to show server status notification.

All permissions are requested only when needed and can be revoked by the user at any time through device settings.

Data Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device.
  • No data is transmitted over the internet (except when you actively use the FTP server to transfer files).
  • SSL/TLS encryption (FTPS) is available for secure file transfers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking services are used.

Third-Party Services

Air FTP Server does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app uses open-source libraries (Apache FTP Server, BouncyCastle) for core functionality, which do not collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we do not collect any data, this is not applicable.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: hello@ksnxrl.dev
  • Developer: KSNXRL

Your Consent

By using Air FTP Server, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

Note: This app is a local FTP server that runs entirely on your device. All file transfers occur directly between your device and connected FTP clients. We do not have access to any of your files or data.
